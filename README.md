# Healthcare Performance Analysis Data Story

**Analyst:** 22f2000771@ds.study.iitm.ac.in

## Executive Summary
This analysis reviews the 2024 quarterly patient satisfaction scores to identify trends and align with the industry benchmark of 4.5.

## Key Findings
* **Volatile Performance:** The scores show extreme volatility, ranging from a low of **3.01** (Q3) to a high of **9.67** (Q4).
* **Average Performance:** Despite the dips in Q1 and Q3, the strong rebound in Q4 has pulled the annual average up to **5.47**.
* **Benchmark Comparison:** The current average (5.47) is **above** the industry target (4.5), largely driven by the Q4 outlier.

## Business Implications
While the average looks good on paper, the inconsistency suggests underlying operational instability. The low scores in Q1 and Q3 indicate periods where patient trust was compromised.

## Recommendations
To stabilize satisfaction scores and ensure consistent performance above the 4.5 benchmark, we recommend the following solution:

**"improve service quality and wait times"**

By standardizing triage protocols and increasing staffing during peak hours, we can prevent the dips seen in Q1 and Q3.
